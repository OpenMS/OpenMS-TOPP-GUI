The OpenMS GUI is an add-on to OpenMS: it is installed into the folder of the OpenMS installation of the same version, which has to be installed first (for all users, in /Applications). TOPPView, TOPPAS and INIFileEditor are added there as applications; ExecutePipeline and ImageCreator join the other TOPP tools in its bin folder.

For more information visit our website at www.OpenMS.de.
